How a cleanup works

1. Collect

Teams of ten people walk along one part of the shore with bags and litter pickers. A team leader takes care of sharp or dangerous things.

2. Sort and count

We put everything in groups: recycling, reuse and trash. Then we count it before we go home.

3. Hand it over

The sorted things go to recycling companies. We also send our numbers to the city.
